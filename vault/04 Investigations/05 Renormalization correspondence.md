# Investigation 05: Renormalization correspondence

## Delivery card

[D025 — Decide whether an RG mapping exists](../../plan/deliverables/D025-rg-decision.md)

## Goal

Try to build an exact correspondence between the proposed method and RG. Report a negative result if the required objects do not exist.

## Mandatory sources

- [[01 Concepts/Renormalization comparison]]
- Wilson and Kogut in [[06 Sources/Bibliography]]
- [[02 Papers/Marchand 2023 - Wavelet conditional RG]]
- Mehta and Schwab, and Stottmeister et al. in [[06 Sources/Bibliography]]

## Tasks

1. Identify fine and coarse degrees of freedom.
2. Define coarse-graining, marginalization, and rescaling operators.
3. Identify running parameters and a semigroup or repeated transformation.
4. Determine the direction of the interpolation recurrence relative to RG.
5. Search for fixed points, invariants, and relevant directions.
6. Test an analysis/synthesis dual interpretation.
7. Compare deterministic interpolation with conditional probabilistic reconstruction.

## Deliverables

- Dictionary mapping RG terms to interpolation objects.
- Commutative diagram if an exact mapping exists.
- First failed axiom if it does not.
- Recommended wording: exact RG, reverse RG, RG-inspired, or no RG claim.

## Stop condition

Stop when the mapping either preserves the transformation structure or fails at a named, demonstrated step.
