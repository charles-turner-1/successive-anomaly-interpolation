## Output contract for every investigator

- Write claims as **established**, **derived**, **empirical**, or **conjectural**.
- Link every literature claim to the original paper or official repository.
- Quote equations by number when possible.
- Provide one strongest match and one decisive mismatch.
- Record negative results.
- End with a recommendation: pursue, modify, merge with known method, or drop.
- Name the hypothesis IDs tested, restate their frozen success and failure criteria, and report the verdict without changing thresholds.
- Use [[Templates/Investigation result template]].
