# Bibliography

## Multilevel interpolation and approximation

- Lee, S., Wolberg, G., and Shin, S. Y. (1997). Scattered data interpolation with multilevel B-splines. *IEEE TVCG*, 3(3), 228-244. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/2945.620490). See [[02 Papers/Lee 1997 - Multilevel B-splines]].
- Floater, M. S., and Iske, A. (1996). Multistep scattered data interpolation using compactly supported radial basis functions. *Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics*, 73, 65-78. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0377-0427(96)00035-0).
- Narcowich, F. J., Schaback, R., and Ward, J. D. (1999). Multilevel interpolation and approximation. *Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis*, 7(3), 243-261. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/acha.1999.0269). See [[02 Papers/Narcowich Schaback Ward 1999 - Multilevel interpolation]].
- Wendland, H. (2010). Multiscale analysis in Sobolev spaces on bounded domains. *Numerische Mathematik*, 116, 493-517. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00211-010-0313-8).
- Townsend, A., and Wendland, H. (2013). Multiscale analysis in Sobolev spaces on bounded domains with zero boundary values. *IMA Journal of Numerical Analysis*, 33(3), 1095-1114. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/imanum/drs036).
- Georgoulis, E. H., Levesley, J., and Subhan, F. (2013). Multilevel sparse kernel-based interpolation. *SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing*, 35(2), A815-A831. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/110859610). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.4153).
- Ding, S., Xia, B., and Bu, D. (2023). Accurate interpolation for scattered data through hierarchical residual refinement. *NeurIPS 2023*. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/1d5a92867cf463fad136cfa23395840b-Abstract.html). [Code](https://github.com/DingShizhe/HINT).

- Daubechies, I. (1988). Orthonormal bases of compactly supported wavelets. *Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics*, 41(7), 909-996. [PDF](https://math.duke.edu/~ingrid/publications/cpam41-1988.pdf).
- Mallat, S. G. (1989). Multiresolution approximations and wavelet orthonormal bases of \(L^2(\mathbb R)\). *Transactions of the AMS*, 315(1), 69-87. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1989-1008470-5).
- Mallat, S. G. (1989). A theory for multiresolution signal decomposition: the wavelet representation. *IEEE TPAMI*, 11(7), 674-693. [Publication page](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/biblio.html).
- Mallat, S. G., and Zhang, Z. (1993). Matching pursuits with time-frequency dictionaries. *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing*, 41(12), 3397-3415. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/78.258082).
- Coifman, R. R., and Maggioni, M. (2006). Diffusion wavelets. *Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis*, 21(1), 53-94. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acha.2006.04.004).

- Troupin, C., Barth, A., Sirjacobs, D., Ouberdous, M., Brankart, J.-M., Brasseur, P., Rixen, M., Alvera-Azcárate, A., Belounis, M., Capet, A., Lenartz, F., Toussaint, M.-E., and Beckers, J.-M.
