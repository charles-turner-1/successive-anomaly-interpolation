# Narcowich, Schaback, and Ward 1999: Multilevel interpolation and approximation

**Citation:** F. J. Narcowich, R. Schaback, and J. D. Ward. Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis 7(3), 243-261, 1999. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/acha.1999.0269).

## Why it matters

This paper is a foundational reference for multilevel kernel and RBF approximation. Later multiscale RBF papers cite it when analyzing levelwise interpolation and approximation.

## Questions for a full read

- What precise sequence of spaces and data sets is assumed?
- Does the correction operator interpolate the residual or quasi-interpolate it?
- Which norm contracts at each stage?
- How do fill distance, separation radius, and kernel scale enter the bounds?
- Are the level spaces nested, or is convergence proved without nesting?

## Relation to the project

This paper is the right place to test whether "successive anomaly interpolation" already has a standard approximation-theory name. It may also provide the proof skeleton for convergence without requiring orthogonal wavelet projections.
