## Reading rule

Each investigation should record:

- exact operator recurrence;
- approximation space at each level;
- how scale changes;
- convergence or stability theorem and its assumptions;
- computational complexity;
- treatment of noise;
- closest point of overlap with the proposed method;
- one decisive difference.
