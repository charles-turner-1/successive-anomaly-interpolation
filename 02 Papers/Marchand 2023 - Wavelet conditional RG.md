# Marchand et al. 2023: Wavelet conditional renormalization group

**Citation:** Tanguy Marchand, Misaki Ozawa, Giulio Biroli, and Stéphane Mallat. Physical Review X 13, 041038, 2023. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.13.041038). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04941).

## Why it matters

WC-RG is the strongest source linking the fields named in the shared conversation. It estimates high-dimensional field distributions scale by scale, represents interactions in an orthogonal wavelet basis, and models fast degrees of freedom conditioned on coarse fields. Sampling then proceeds from coarse to fine.

## Precise overlap

- Wavelet separation of scales.
- Coarse-to-fine conditional generation.
- Decomposition into interaction energies across scales.
- Explicit RG interpretation with physical fields.

## Precise difference

WC-RG learns a probability distribution and energy function, not merely a deterministic interpolant through scattered observations. Its "residual" objects are conditional fast variables, not ordinary pointwise errors.

## Research opportunity

If the proposed method includes uncertainty or conditional distributions for anomalies, WC-RG may supply a much stronger theory than deterministic residual fitting. If it only outputs a mean surface, use it as a conceptual boundary rather than the main precedent.
