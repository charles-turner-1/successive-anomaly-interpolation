# Investigation 10: Novelty and terminology search

## Goal

Find the accepted names for the method and prevent an avoidable novelty collision.

## Search families

- multilevel interpolation and approximation;
- multiscale RBF interpolation;
- residual correction and subspace correction;
- multilevel B-spline approximation;
- hierarchical residual refinement;
- progressive iterative approximation;
- kernel matching pursuit and greedy kernel approximation;
- multilevel sparse kernel interpolation;
- Laplacian pyramid reconstruction;
- cascade correlation and stagewise additive models;
- reverse or inverse renormalization;
- spectral residual interpolation.

## Method

1. Search titles and abstracts.
2. Follow references backward from the five Priority A papers.
3. Follow citing papers forward.
4. Search patents only after the academic terminology stabilizes.
5. Record exact equation matches, not word matches.

## Deliverables

- Terminology graph with synonyms and distinct meanings.
- Earliest exact recurrence found.
- Most recent competitive implementation.
- Draft related-work paragraph.
- Novelty risk rated high, medium, or low with evidence.
