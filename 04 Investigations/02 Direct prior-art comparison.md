# Investigation 02: Direct prior-art comparison

## Goal

Determine whether the proposed recurrence is equivalent to, a special case of, or materially different from established multilevel interpolation.

## Mandatory sources

- [[02 Papers/Lee 1997 - Multilevel B-splines]]
- [[02 Papers/Narcowich Schaback Ward 1999 - Multilevel interpolation]]
- [[02 Papers/Wendland 2010 - Multiscale RBF approximation]]
- [[02 Papers/Georgoulis 2013 - Multilevel sparse kernel interpolation]]
- [[02 Papers/Ding 2023 - HINT]]

## Tasks

For every source, extract the recurrence, level space, site hierarchy, scale rule, convergence result, complexity, and noise treatment. Normalize all algorithms into the notation of [[00 Project/Working definition and assumptions]].

Build an equivalence table. A difference counts as material only if it changes the hypothesis class, objective, convergence assumptions, computational order, uncertainty model, or observed behavior.

## Deliverables

- Algorithm-by-algorithm normalized equations.
- Nearest-prior-art ranking with reasons.
- Candidate novelty statement that survives the comparison.
- A list of claims that must not be made.

## Stop condition

Stop after backward and forward citation searches cease producing a closer recurrence in two consecutive search rounds.
